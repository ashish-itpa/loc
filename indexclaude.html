Inside a Little Owls Session

A Little Owls session feels like storytime, game time and "a-ha!" time rolled into one.

1

Warm-Up

We start with a quick warm-up to settle bodies and minds.

2

Spark Curiosity

A story, challenge or real-life scenario sparks curiosity.

3

Active Play

Children work in teams, role-play, build, decide and solve.

4

Reflection Circle

Children name what they felt, learnt and want to try next.

It's structured, but it feels like play.

Our Curriculum

Three interconnected pillars

Thinking Skills

  • • Problem-solving
  • • Decision-making
  • • Planning and sequencing
  • • Critical thinking
  • • Creative thinking

Social Skills

  • • Listening and speaking
  • • Taking turns and sharing
  • • Teamwork and collaboration
  • • Conflict resolution
  • • Empathy & perspective-taking

Emotional Skills

  • • Recognising feelings
  • • Managing frustration
  • • Building confidence
  • • Handling mistakes
  • • Self-awareness

These pillars aren't taught separately. In every session, children engage all three — thinking through a challenge, working with others, and managing their emotions along the way.

About the LOC Team

We're a team of mothers on a mission!!

Mothers who bring decades of corporate and entrepreneurial experience — and a shared passion for building future-ready, confident young learners.

One of our founders has formally studied Early Childhood Education the Finnish way through HY+ (University of Helsinki), globally recognised as the world's leading model for early learning. This brings deep, research-backed insight into how children actually learn — through joy, curiosity, choice, and exploration.

Little Owls Club is built to blend global pedagogy and real parenting wisdom.

  • On our belief that childhood should feel joyful.
  • On our passion to nurture curious, resilient, empathetic thinkers.
  • And on our conviction that strong foundations built early, can shape a child for life.

Every program we run is thoughtfully designed: rooted in evidence-based learning, infused with heart, and driven by our commitment to create real impact — one child and one meaningful moment at a time.
